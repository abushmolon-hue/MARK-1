About

The name means red beard, and the kitchen follows the flag: dough proves for 48 hours, the oven runs on wood, and the menu goes from breakfast cornetto to a midnight Diavola without losing its nerve.

Breakfast and coffee in the morning. Pasta, salads, poke and burgers at lunch. Pizza, wine and cocktails from 5pm. If you only know us for one thing, come argue with us about the La Barbarossa.
